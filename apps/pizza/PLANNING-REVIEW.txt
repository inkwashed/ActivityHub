PIZZA BUILDER — PLANNING REVIEW, v0.4
Compared with the full “Plan Pizza Builder” conversation.

IMPLEMENTED
- Pizza base, three sauces, and all seven originally agreed toppings.
- Optional sauce, dry-crust reset, and a continuous spreading animation.
- Sausage and pepperoni added in v0.4; nine toppings total, cheese first.
- Three quantity levels, small state dots, fourth-tap removal, and Clear Pizza.
- Sprinkled placement that preserves earlier pieces, rotation/scale variation,
  ingredient-specific quantities, and fuller shredded-cheese clusters.
- Picture-and-English-label buttons, “What do you want?” and “I want ...”.
- Original artwork, no login or student data, no external dependencies.
- Separate recipe configuration and placement engine.
- Responsive layout, compact tablet controls, and natural scrolling on phones.

DISCUSSED BUT NOT YET IMPLEMENTED
- Direct removal by tapping an ingredient on the pizza, or a separate remove/
  undo control. Current removal is the fourth button tap or Clear Pizza.
- A Done! view showing the finished pizza cleanly.
- Optional audio prompts, teacher mode, and random pizza challenges.
- Optional hiding of English labels or quantity dots, repositioning pieces,
  and multiple artwork variants per ingredient. These were exploratory ideas.
- Installable PWA with offline caching after a website visit. The downloaded
  files already work offline in a desktop browser, but this is not a PWA.
- The wider Student Tools landing page, additional builders, and hosting setup.
- A fully general shared framework: recipe data is configurable now, but the
  circular placement boundary, page wording, and base styling still need
  adapting for a parfait glass or other builder. A second app would test this.

Earlier removal suggestions were alternatives to the agreed tap cycle.
Bacon appeared among brainstormed examples, not the final seven-topping scope;
the user has now rounded out the list with sausage and pepperoni.

VALIDATION
Logic checks cover all nine quantity cycles, retained placements, sauce changes,
spiral progression/cancellation, reduced-motion behavior, and reset.
Tablet styling targets 768 x 1024 and 1024 x 768, with extra allowance for browser
controls (768 x 900 and 1024 x 650 content areas). No overflow is hidden to force
fit. Physical iPad/browser layout and animation verification remain outstanding;
the earlier local-file browser preview was blocked by browser security policy.

Update v0.12: The Share photo preview now supplies a finished-pizza display and
PNG download. The earlier Done-view suggestion is partially covered by this.
