# Sandwich Builder

Follow the root workflow and style guide. Listed on the homepage under NEW HORIZON Elementary 6, Unit 5. Ingredient/source mappings belong in config.js; never infer a selected source. LEARN uses three explicit slots, fixed bread, initials in slot order, and optional source sentences. PLAY uses the shared creative editor and the same per-filling-type country rule as LEARN. Difficulty is independent of build mode; HARD adds the chosen origins and EASY omits them. Bread has no origin. Target grammar follows the user's instructions rather than the PDF's title. Do not copy textbook illustrations.
